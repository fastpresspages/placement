Privacy

Effective 26 October 2026. Short version: no analytics, no cookies, no local storage, no trackers. We only see what you deliberately send us.

What we collect

The suggestion form sends your email and message to FormSubmit, which forwards it to meetcode99@gmail.com. That's the whole pipeline. GitHub logs standard request metadata for the Pages site; we don't access those logs.

Fonts

Typography loads from Google Fonts. That's the only third-party request besides FormSubmit.

Deletion

Email meetcode99@gmail.com and we will delete your correspondence. We are a small open-source team, not a data broker.
